### 🧩 About

Masters student in AI with an engineering background in Electronics & Telecommunication, graduating **December 2027**. Most of what I build sits at the point where a model stops being a demo and has to actually work — handling inputs longer than the context window, validating what comes back, and failing in ways a user can understand.

- 🔭 Building **LLM applications** with locally hosted models (Ollama, Llama 3.1, Gemma 3)
- 🧠 Into **RAG, agent workflows and LLM evaluation** — measuring quality, not just shipping prompts
- 🛠️ Previously: multilingual OCR pipelines, ML prediction web apps, market segmentation
- 📍 Melbourne, Australia — open to AI / ML opportunities
